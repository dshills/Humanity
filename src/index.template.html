Reading the timeline

Click the axis
Zoom in four times around that moment. Keep clicking: years become months become days. On the widest views a bracket at the right edge jumps straight to recorded history.
Shift+click · - · Zoom out
Step back out. 0 or Home returns to all of humanity.
Wheel · pinch · drag
Zoom around the pointer and pan. The strip under the header shows where you are; click or drag it to jump.
Click an event
Open its panel: a map, the story, links, what else happened around then, sometimes a museum object. Copy link shares that exact event. Zoomed in to a month or less, you can load Wikipedia's day-by-day events.
/ Search
Find any of the events by name and jump to it.
Legend
Show or hide categories (Shift+click shows one alone) and filter by region.
